friendlychaos

about

Originally rippin’ from Edmonton, Alberta, Sean Bishop started friendlychaos as an indie solo project after living life on the road in his teeny-tiny SUV, “Ips,” lovingly named after the genus of bark beetles.

Singing groovy tunes somewhere between Mac DeMarco, Tom Misch, and Cage the Elephant with little jazzy jazz sprinkles on top. Expect witty banter, some yelping and hollering, and an intensity of songwriting that keeps you on your toes.

Behind the silliness, friendlychaos writes straight from the gut about love lost and earned, unfiltered longing, and the natural world that inspires him.

Weathered by the wild Yukon, carried across Idaho’s sagebrush steppe and down the river-fed mountains of Missoula, friendlychaos now calls Minneapolis, Minnesota home.
